About the Project

What this analysis covers

This project analyses historical Olympic data to understand who participates, who wins, and which sports and countries dominate. It looks at the physical profile of medal winners (age, height, weight), the growth of participation by gender, and the split between Summer and Winter Games. The dashboard is built in Tableau and embedded below so you can filter and explore it directly.

Key Insights

What the dashboard shows

Winner profile

Most medal winners are in their mid-20s to early 30s, with weight clustered around 60–85 kg and height around 170–185 cm.

Top sports

Athletics leads with about 3,969 medals, followed by Swimming and Rowing (about 2,945), with Gymnastics, Fencing and Cycling also near the top.

Top countries

The USA leads by a wide margin with about 5,637 medals, followed by the former USSR, Germany and Great Britain.

Participation trends

Total athletes grew strongly over time, and female participation rose sharply after 1960. Summer Games are far larger than Winter Games.
